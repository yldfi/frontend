https://gov.curve.finance/t/llamalend-v1-market-deprecation-plan/11075

DAO proposal:
https://www.curve.finance/dao/ethereum/proposals/1422-ownership

yldfi.co/vaults/yscvxcrv

4/ Once LlamaLend v2 is live on mainnet later in 2026, yld will explore launching a new ycvxCRV vault designed for the v2 market.

Thank you to everyone who used ycvxCRV to borrow against their staked cvxCRV.
